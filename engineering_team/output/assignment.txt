Assignment: Simple Fractions 

Objective: 
To understand and apply the concept of simple fractions.  

Instructions:

1. Write the fraction 1/2 as a percentage.
2. Write the fraction 3/5 as a percentage.
3. If it takes 5/6 of a meter of wire to make a toy, how much more meter wire is required to make one more toy using the 3 meter wire available?

Tasks:

1. Show your work or reasoning for each problem.
2. Draw a picture to illustrate your answer to problem 3.

(Note: Please answer all the tasks on a separate sheet of paper and submit it along with this assignment and a simple drawing that represents a scenario using simple fractions.)

**You can use a pencil or a pen to complete this assignment, but make sure to show all your work and reasoning for each problem.**
