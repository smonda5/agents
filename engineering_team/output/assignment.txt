### Math Adventure Assignment: "The Magical Animal Kingdom!"

Welcome to the Magical Animal Kingdom! In this land filled with friendly animals, you will help them solve fun math puzzles to earn your "Animal Helper Badge!" Let's get started on your adventure!

#### Instructions:
1. Complete all the problems.
2. Write your answers clearly in the space provided.
3. Don’t forget to use your imagination! Feel free to draw pictures or create your own story related to the problems.

**1. Addition Quest:**  
*The Lion is planning a big party! He invited friends from different parts of the kingdom!*

- 7 Elephant friends + 5 Giraffe friends = _____ friends coming to the party!

- 3 Monkey friends + 6 Zebra friends = _____ friends coming to the party!

#### Draw a picture of the party!

---

**2. Subtraction Challenge:**  
*Oh no! The Squirrel had to leave the party early! Can you figure out how many friends are still there?*

- There were 10 birds at the party. 4 flew away!  
  10 - 4 = _____ birds left.

- The Lion had 12 cupcakes. He ate 3 cupcakes!  
  12 - 3 = _____ cupcakes left.

#### Draw a cupcake for each one left!

---

**3. Multiplication Adventure:**  
*The animals want to set up tables for the food. Each table can fit 3 plates. Can you figure out how many plates are needed for 4 tables?*

- 3 plates/table × 4 tables = _____ plates needed!

#### Color in the plates!

---

**4. Word Problem Wonderland:**  
*Help the Owl solve these problems to unlock the treasure chest!*

- If the Wolf has 5 apples and gathers 3 more, how many apples does he have altogether?  
  _____ apples.

- The Rabbit found 8 carrots but lost 2. How many carrots does he have now?  
  _____ carrots.

---

**5. Animal Patterns:**  
*The Parrot loves patterns! Can you help him complete these patterns?*

- 🦁, 🦓, 🦁, 🦓, _____, _____

- 🌟, 🌟, 🌙, 🌟, 🌟, _____, _____

#### Draw your own pattern using animals!

---

**6. Math Game: Tally Time!**  
*Count your favorite animals and make a tally for each!*

- How many Dogs do you see? ___ (tally them!)
- How many Cats do you see? ___ (tally them!)
- How many Birds do you see? ___ (tally them!)

#### Count all your tallies and write them here:  
Dogs: _____  
Cats: _____  
Birds: _____

---

### Bonus Challenge: Create Your Own Story!  
Imagine you are an animal in the Magical Kingdom. Write a short story (4-5 sentences) about your day and include at least two addition or subtraction problems!

---

**Finish your adventure!**  
Once you've completed this assignment, show it to your teacher, and don’t forget to tell them about your Animal Helper Badge!
